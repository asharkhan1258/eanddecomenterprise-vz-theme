Because our services involve consulting and project work, refund eligibility depends on the stage of delivery.

Before Work Begins

If a prepaid service is cancelled before work has started, the client may request a full refund of the amount paid for that service.

After Work Begins

If work has started but the agreed service has not been completed, any refund may be reduced to account for work already performed, non-refundable third-party costs, or delivered materials.

After Delivery

Once the agreed service or deliverable has been completed and supplied, payments are generally non-refundable unless otherwise required by applicable law or agreed in writing.

Company-Initiated Cancellation

If we cancel a service before completing it, the unused portion of prepaid fees will be refunded to the original payment method where reasonably possible.
